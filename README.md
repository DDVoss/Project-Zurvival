# Survival Horror Prototype – Proof of Concept
Overview

This project is a small proof-of-concept demo exploring core mechanics of a survival horror game. The focus has been on building foundational systems such as player movement, camera design, and basic enemy behavior, rather than creating a full game experience.

The prototype demonstrates core concepts like  slow and methodical controls with simple AI can create tension while using fixed camera perspective to give limited visibility.

## Inspiration

The project draws inspiration from classic survival horror titles, particularly:

Resident Evil – for its fixed camera angles, limited player control, and tension through restricted information

Eternal Darkness: Sanity's Requiem – for its cinematic camera framing

Signalis – for its modern interpretation of classic survival horror mechanics and atmosphere

## Game Objective

The goal of the player is to navigate through confined environments while avoiding or confronting hostile enemies. Due to limited visibility and constrained camera angles, the player must proceed carefully, often committing to movement without full knowledge of what lies ahead.
Due to its slow pace, players are encouraged to think about how to deal with encounters with limited resources like healing and ammunition.
